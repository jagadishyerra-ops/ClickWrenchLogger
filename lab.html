Click sound: not taught

Press Teach, then make 10 real clicks on this wrench. The Lab learns what this wrench's click sounds like, then suggests sound: click on every new clip. You still choose the label; press Enter to accept a suggestion.

Doing a run of one thing (say 20 clicks)? Tick auto-label, pick the class, then just do them. Classes click and pull-through count as a click; drop raises the drop flag; everything else is ignored.

what makes a clip

With the box ticked, a clip stays open until the wrench has been quiet for the "After" time, so the click and the return click when the head springs back land in one clip (marked click and return). Raise "After" if your return clicks still come out as separate clips.

Dataset

Aim for at least 30 clips per class from at least three different wrenches (one model serves every wrench). Clips stay in this browser on this laptop; export the dataset to back it up or to merge clips from other laptops.

Train

Training uses only labelled clips. It runs five rounds, each time holding back a fifth of the clips the model has never seen, so the scores below are what to expect on new movements, not a memory test. The pod's current fixed rules are scored on the same clips for comparison.
